Technical Overview

FRP Poles

Kriza FRP Light Poles are high-strength composite poles designed to survive severe coastal winds, salt spray, and extreme weather without rusting or degrading. Engineered with polyurethane UV coatings and dielectrically safe materials, our poles eliminate electrocution hazards for public and industrial lighting.
