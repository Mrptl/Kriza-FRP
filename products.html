FRP Cable Trays

Ladder and Perforated type cable management systems offering excellent electrical insulation. Compliant with NEMA FG-1-1993 for electrical and instrumentation cable laying in power, telecom, and chemical sectors.

Fire Retardant

FRP Gratings

High-strength, corrosion-resistant Molded and Pultruded gratings engineered for walkways, trench covers, and platforms. Compliant with OSHA standards for chemical, petrochemical, and pharmaceutical industries.

Heavy Duty

PP FRP Tanks

Custom-engineered storage tanks in PP-FRP, PVC-FRP, and HDPE-FRP. Designed to ASME RTP 1 and BS-EN-13121-3 standards for maximum durability in acid, chemical, and water treatment applications.

FRP Profiles

Pultruded structural sections offering high strength-to-weight ratios for custom fabrications, platforms, and skids. Manufactured to ANSI and OSHA standards for demanding industrial environments.

Featured

FRP Walkways & Platforms

Complete structural walkway, staircase, and safety railing systems built with FRP materials. Ensuring OSHA-compliant safety, slip resistance, and absolute immunity to corrosion.

FRP Pipes & Fittings

Filament-wound fiberglass reinforced pipes designed to handle corrosive chemicals, sewage, industrial effluents, and high-pressure water transmission. Built to ASME and ASTM standards.

FRP Poles

Non-conductive, maintenance-free FRP/GRP Light Poles. Built to ANSI C 136 and BS EN 40-7 standards to withstand extreme coastal and offshore weather conditions without rusting.

FRP Sheets

Corrosion-proof, weather-resistant, and high-strength FRP sheets. Available in corrugated, flat, and gritted profiles for industrial roofing, cladding, partition walls, and protective linings.

FRP Canopies

Heavy-duty protective canopies designed to shelter outdoor industrial equipment, instruments, push buttons, and motors from extreme weather, UV exposure, and corrosive chemical drops.

FRP Rods

High-performance pultruded solid FRP rods engineered for structural reinforcement, electrical insulation, and high tensile strength. Ideal for antennas, transformer spacer rods, utility poles, and marine structures.

FRP Ladder

Designed for demanding industrial environments, KRIZA FRP Ladders combine lightweight construction with superior mechanical strength. Unlike conventional steel ladders, they never rust or corrode, reducing maintenance costs and extending service life.
