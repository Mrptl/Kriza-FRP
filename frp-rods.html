Technical Overview

FRP Rods

Kriza FRP Rods are continuous pultruded solid glass fiber reinforced rods engineered for applications demanding extreme tensile strength, high flexural strength, and superior electrical insulation. They serve as ideal replacements for steel and wood in electrical, marine, and antenna applications.
